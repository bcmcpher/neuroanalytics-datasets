# Credit and Contributions

Modern research products are built by more people than an author list can express, and
"who did what" degrades quickly once a project spans years and institutions.

Two standards address this. **ORCID** gives every researcher a persistent identifier, so
your work stays attached to you across name changes and institutions. **CRediT** is a
14-role taxonomy — conceptualization, software, data curation, writing, and so on — that
records contribution explicitly rather than encoding it in author order.

Both matter more for the kind of work in this program, where a substantial contribution may
be a pipeline or a released dataset rather than a paragraph of a manuscript.
