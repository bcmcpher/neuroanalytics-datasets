# Aging and Cognitive Decline

Not all cognitive change with age is disease. A recurring question across these projects
is how to separate normal lifespan variation from the early signal of pathology — which
is why normative modelling and large lifespan cohorts appear so often alongside the
clinical datasets.

## CNS Projects

- [Josh Neudorf](../cohort1/josh_neudorf.md) — structural and functional connectivity
  changes in older age that help preserve cognitive ability, and the effects of acute
  sleep restriction on older adults
- [Sima Abbasi Habashi](../cohort2/sima_abbasi.md) — why white matter hyperintensity
  burden progresses at very different rates across individuals as they age
- [Trishna Saha Detroja](../cohort2/trishna_saha_detroja.md) — sleep fragmentation, brain
  structure, and dementia in aging
- [Mohamed Abdelhack](../cohort1/mohamed_abdelhack.md) — cognitive and motor change in
  neurodegenerative and vascular disease affecting older Canadians
