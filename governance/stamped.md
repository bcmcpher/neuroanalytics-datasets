# STAMPED Principles

STAMPED is a set of seven principles for research objects — **S**elf-containment,
**T**racking, **A**ctionability, **M**odularity, **P**ortability, **E**phemerality,
**D**istributability — intended as a practical successor to FAIR for work that is meant to
be *built upon* rather than merely found.

The distinction that matters: FAIR asks whether someone can locate and access your data.
STAMPED asks whether they can pick it up and keep going — re-execute the analysis,
recombine a component, deploy it somewhere else.

Much of this book already argues for pieces of STAMPED without naming it. Containers are
Portability; DataLad provenance is Tracking; BIDS is Self-containment and Modularity. A
page here would give that scattered advice a single spine.
