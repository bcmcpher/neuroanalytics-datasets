# Pre-registration

Pre-registration means writing down what you intend to test, and how, **before** you look
at the results — then registering that plan somewhere timestamped and public. It is the
main structural defence against analytic flexibility: with enough preprocessing choices and
model specifications, a large neuroimaging dataset will produce a significant result for
almost any hypothesis.

It also draws a clear line between confirmatory and exploratory work, which lets you
explore freely without that exploration quietly being reported as confirmation.
