# Parkinson's Disease

Both CNS projects on Parkinson's disease use **normative modelling** — building a
reference distribution of brain measures across a healthy population, then quantifying
how far an individual patient deviates from it. This suits PD well, because the
neuropsychiatric symptoms that matter clinically vary enormously between individuals and
are poorly captured by group averages.

## CNS Projects

- [Julia-Katharina Pfarr](../cohort1/julia-katharina_pfarr.md) — normative modelling to
  predict neuropsychiatric symptoms in PD from brain imaging that is already collected in
  routine clinical practice, adapting the Rutherford et al. lifespan reference model
- [Johanna Bayer](../cohort2/johannaBayer.md) — predicting PD progression using
  longitudinal, federated normative modelling, a methodology she developed to
  characterise individual trajectories of brain change
