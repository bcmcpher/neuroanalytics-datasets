# Reporting Guidelines

Reporting guidelines are checklists of what a paper must state for a reader to judge, and
a peer to reproduce, what was done. They exist because the same omissions recur — the
smoothing kernel that went unreported, the exclusions applied after seeing the results, the
validation set that was not really held out.

The ones relevant to work in this program:

- **COBIDAS** — the OHBM committee's reporting standards for MRI and MEEG studies
- **TRIPOD** — for clinical prediction models, which covers a substantial share of the
  projects here
- **STROBE** — observational studies
- **CLAIM** — AI in medical imaging
