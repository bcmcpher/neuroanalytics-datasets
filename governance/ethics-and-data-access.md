# Research Ethics and Data Access

This book documents *which* datasets exist. It does not yet document how to get into them,
and that access process is usually the longest-lead item in a new project.

Open data is rarely open in the unconditional sense. Most of the datasets listed here sit
somewhere on a spectrum from "download it now" to "submit a proposal, sign a Data Use
Agreement, and wait." Some require REB approval at your institution even for secondary
analysis; some require your supervisor as a named applicant; some require a specific
compute environment.
