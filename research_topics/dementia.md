# Dementia

Dementia is a clinical syndrome with several underlying causes — Alzheimer's pathology,
vascular damage, and others — which is precisely what makes it hard to predict. Several
CNS projects target the transition point: identifying who will progress from mild
cognitive impairment to dementia, and which biological pathways drive that progression.

## CNS Projects

- [Josh Neudorf](../cohort1/josh_neudorf.md) — graph neural network deep learning to
  predict progression from mild cognitive impairment to dementia from brain network
  configuration
- [Mohamed Abdelhack](../cohort1/mohamed_abdelhack.md) — how cardiovascular illness may
  predispose individuals to dementia at the level of brain function
- [Sima Abbasi Habashi](../cohort2/sima_abbasi.md) — genetic and immune pathways driving
  white matter hyperintensity burden, a strong predictor of dementia
- [Tom George](../cohort2/tom_george.md) — foundation models fine-tuned on clinical tasks
  with a focus on dementia prediction
- [Trishna Saha Detroja](../cohort2/trishna_saha_detroja.md) — the bidirectional
  relationship between sleep and dementia at the cellular level
- [Lindsay Munroe](../cohort1/lindsay_munroe.md) — predicting progression of
  neurodegenerative disease with explainable AI
