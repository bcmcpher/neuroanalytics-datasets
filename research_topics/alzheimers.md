# Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer's disease is the single most common thread running through CNS scholar
projects. The work below approaches it from very different angles — electrophysiology,
functional connectivity, graph learning, transfer learning — but shares a motivation:
detecting change **before** clinical symptoms appear, using data already collected in
routine practice.

## CNS Projects

- [Diellor Basha](../cohort1/diellor_basha.md) — electrophysiological biomarkers of
  Abeta/tau burden in preclinical AD, combining MEG/EEG with MRI, PET and CSF measures
  from [PREVENT-AD](../data/databases/prevent-ad.md)
- [Davor Curic](../cohort1/davor_curic.md) — geometry-aware (tangent space) analysis of
  how the functional connectome moves over time, to track disease progression
- [Hao-Ting Wang](../cohort1/hao-ting_wang.md) — predicting presymptomatic features of AD
  with transfer learning, pooling large datasets to pretrain a model for a smaller
  clinical cohort
- [Josh Neudorf](../cohort1/josh_neudorf.md) — graph neural network deep learning to find
  connectivity biomarkers of progression from mild cognitive impairment to dementia
- [Mohamed Abdelhack](../cohort1/mohamed_abdelhack.md) — shared and unique patterns of
  brain function between neurodegenerative and cardiovascular disease
- [Tom George](../cohort2/tom_george.md) — a neuro-foundation model pretrained on open
  data, fine-tuned for neurodegenerative disease prediction
- [Trishna Saha Detroja](../cohort2/trishna_saha_detroja.md) — sleep-related cortical
  cell-type subpopulations and their link to dementia-related brain change
